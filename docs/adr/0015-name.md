# ADR 0015 — The product is named Lumi

**Status:** Accepted, with recorded risk · 2026-08-07

## Context

Four naming directions were considered: literal (*Skill Manager*), map metaphor (*Atlas*,
*Survey*), place metaphor (*Depot*, *Locker*), and short/abstract (the Raycast/Fig/Warp lane).

Short and abstract was chosen — it is the most brandable and the most defensible, and both
matter for a product with a landing page and a portfolio role.

## Decision

**Lumi.** Project directory renamed from the `skills-manager` placeholder.

## Recorded risks

Checked 2026-08-07:

| Check | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Homebrew cask/formula | **Clear.** Nearest neighbours are `lumide`, `luminance-hdr`, `lume` |
| Domain | **Not available** |
| npm package `lumi` | **Taken** — irrelevant for a Mac app, relevant if a CLI companion ever ships |
| USPTO | **LUMI filed in Computer & Software Services by Eluum LLC, 10 April 2026** — live/pending, not yet in use in commerce |
| Existing companies | **Lumi**, Series A AI storytelling platform (Las Vegas, founded 2023). Further LUMI marks exist for lighting and social networking |

The name is crowded, and there is a pending mark in the exact class this product would
occupy.

**Practical risk is currently low** because V1 ships free with a tip jar (ADR 0009) and no
commercial use. The risk becomes real if the product is ever monetised — most plausibly via
the V2 org tier, which is the point at which a proper trademark search is warranted.

Renaming is cheap now and expensive after a landing page, a domain purchase, a Homebrew
cask, and a bundle identifier are in circulation. Accepted knowingly rather than by default.

## Follow-ups

- Pick a domain variant, since the exact match is unavailable.
- Choose a reverse-DNS bundle identifier that does not depend on owning the bare domain.
